# Artwork placement brief — review 01

The selected navy/black/gold/blue crowned-gorilla design remains locked. This is an asset-preparation brief, not new master artwork or another moodboard.

| Placement | Existing suggested source width | Needed asset |
|---|---|---|
| Home | 1600 px or more | Standalone crowned-gorilla/blue-light artwork in the approved composition, with clear space for live HTML text. |
| Music | 1200 px or more | Approved headphone-gorilla artwork or owner-selected genuine feature image. |
| Shows, Band, Media, Press, Merch | 1400 px or more | Approved relevant artwork or real photography; no fictitious people, venues, performances or products. |
| Booking | 1000 px or more | Approved band/performance image beside the real inquiry form. |
| Public logo | Crisp source retaining its proportions | Approved PNG/WebP through Site logo draft/preview/publication. The concept wordmark is not automatically an approved master. |

Widths are existing SectionPages guidance, not proof of detail, rights or acceptance. Do not enlarge a small reference crop and call it a production master. Full-frame video stills can be considered for gallery/poster use after identity and publication approval; they do not substitute for crisp logo art, high-detail heroes or dedicated portraits.

The separate conversation packet contains real frame extracts from earlier user media, not generated people. Their band/recording identity and permission remain unconfirmed. Those images and all private source references stay outside this public repository.

Keep actual headings, controls and layouts unchanged. No invented member identities, events, prices, lyrics, policy promises or backing soundtrack are needed for this preparation step.

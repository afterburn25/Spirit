The original gzip download was decompressed. The MMDB database bytes are unmodified.
The temporary transfer chunks under tools/geo-transfer contain those same bytes
encoded with XZ and Base64 for transport. Metadata, retrieval date and checksums
are recorded in country.json. This country-level Lite database estimates network
location and does not establish an individual's physical location.

# Artwork fidelity pass

Branch: `work/artwork-fidelity`

This is a post-1.0 visual asset pass only. Do not redesign the site or reopen completed application modules.

## Locked visual direction

Preserve the approved first Spiritual Gorillaz composite:

- cinematic black/deep-navy environment;
- gold branding and details;
- restrained blue stage/rim lighting;
- crowned-gorilla identity;
- large editorial typography supplied by real HTML/CSS;
- coordinated visual language across Home, Music, Shows, Band, Media, Booking, Press and Merch.

Do not flatten page UI into artwork. Do not add embedded navigation, buttons, headings, fake products, fake shows, synthetic member portraits or demo content.

## Priority 1 — Home hero master

Replace the tiny concept fallback with a reviewed production master through the existing Home page artwork workflow.

Required composition:

- 2000 × 1400 px or larger preferred; 1600 × 1100 minimum;
- crowned gorilla on the right half/right third;
- strong dark negative space on the left for the existing HTML headline and buttons;
- dark black/deep-navy environment;
- restrained blue rim/stage lighting;
- gold crown/detail accents;
- cinematic photographic/illustrative finish, not cartoon styling;
- no text, buttons, navigation, mockup chrome or watermarks;
- keep critical subject detail away from extreme top/bottom/right edges for responsive cropping.

Suggested production filename: `spiritual-gorillaz-hero-master.webp`.

## Priority 2 — Music artwork

Create a coordinated high-resolution Music-page master based on the approved headphone-gorilla direction. It must look like the same visual world as the Home hero rather than a separate redesign.

- dark navy/black base;
- blue performance/studio light;
- controlled gold detail;
- subject weighted right so page copy remains readable;
- no embedded text/UI;
- responsive-safe focal area.

## Priority 3 — Interior page artwork

Produce coordinated art only where it materially strengthens the approved concept. Reuse the same lighting, contrast, texture and gold/blue palette rather than inventing unrelated visual themes.

## Acceptance

Before any production artwork is published:

1. Preview Home at desktop, tablet and approximately 390 px mobile.
2. Confirm the crowned gorilla remains visible and does not collide with the HTML headline/buttons.
3. Confirm the left side remains dark enough for text contrast.
4. Confirm Music artwork crops cleanly across breakpoints.
5. Confirm no rejected mockup or placeholder/demo artwork is published.
6. Preserve the existing owner CMS/branding workflows and all 1.0 functionality.

The current `images/gorilla-hero.webp` and `images/gorilla-music.webp` remain reference/fallback assets until reviewed masters are ready.
